Oil Markets & the Strait of Hormuz

The Strait of Hormuz is a narrow waterway through which roughly 20% of global oil passes. Suppose a geopolitical crisis closes the strait, disrupting global supply. At the same time, the Canadian federal government has imposed an excise tax on oil producers.

Work through the steps below to analyse this market.

Given
Demand: P = 200 − 2Q
Supply: P = 20 + 2Q
Excise tax: t = $40 per unit
1
Find the pre-tax equilibrium
▼

Set demand equal to supply and solve for Q* and P*.

Q* = units
P* = $/unit
Set the two equations equal: 200 − 2Q = 20 + 2Q. Collect Q terms on one side.
// Set D = S
200 − 2Q = 20 + 2Q
180 = 4Q
Q* = 45

// Substitute back
P* = 200 − 2(45) = 200 − 90
P* = 110

Calculate welfare effects
▼

Using the pre- and post-tax equilibria, calculate the welfare areas. Use the triangle and rectangle formulas.

CS = ½ × (200 − Pc) × Q*ₜ. PS = ½ × (Pp − 20) × Q*ₜ. Tax revenue = t × Q*ₜ. DWL = ½ × t × (Q* − Q*ₜ).
